## Summary

`authenticateWebSession` granted the backing API key's raw `scopes_json` (often `["*"]`) instead of constraining browser sessions like native passkey sessions, `requireStaticApiKey` accepted device/unconfirmed keys without `X-Admin-Confirmation: user-approved` or `ADMIN_RATE_LIMITER`, `POST /auth/logout` lacked an Origin/Sec-Fetch-Site check, and lexicographic `expires_at > datetime('now')` mis-evaluated ISO-8601 `T` timestamps on the expiry date.

## Evidence

- `src/worker/auth.ts`: `authenticateWebSession` returned raw `k.scopes_json` and `k.allowed_identities_json`, and used raw string comparison `expires_at > datetime('now')` (`'2026-11-27T00:01:00.000Z' > '2026-11-27 00:50:00'` evaluates true in ASCII because `'T' > ' '`).
- `src/worker/passkeys.ts` and `src/worker/native-auth.ts`: `requireStaticApiKey` did not reject `lm_device_` keys or enforce `X-Admin-Confirmation: user-approved` (HTTP 428), and `webauthn_credentials` queries did not join active `api_keys`.
- `src/worker/passkeys.ts`: `POST /auth/logout` accepted cross-origin POSTs without checking `Origin` or `Sec-Fetch-Site`.

## Risk

Same-day expired keys remained valid until UTC midnight, revoked/expired backing keys still exposed passkey login metadata, and any future web-session route would inherit `keys:write`/`*` privileges.

## Fix and current state

Fixed in `26f4d72` (`src/worker/auth.ts`, `src/worker/passkeys.ts`, `src/worker/native-auth.ts`): normalized all D1 expiry predicates to `datetime(expires_at) > datetime('now')`, constrained `authenticateWebSession` through `constrainedNativeSessionPolicy`, hardened `requireStaticApiKey` (rejecting `lm_device_` and identity-restricted keys, requiring `keys:write` + `X-Admin-Confirmation: user-approved` -> 428 + `ADMIN_RATE_LIMITER`), joined `webauthn_credentials` against active `api_keys`, and enforced `Origin`/`Sec-Fetch-Site` on `POST /auth/logout`.

## How to verify
