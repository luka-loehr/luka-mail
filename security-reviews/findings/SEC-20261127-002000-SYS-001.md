## Summary

In `ios/LukaMail`, `MailHTMLDocument` stripped only `<script>`, `<iframe>`, `<object>`, and `<embed>` before rendering email HTML in `WKWebView` (leaving `<base>`, `<meta http-equiv="refresh">`, `<svg>`/`<foreignObject>`, `<form>`, `<video>`/`<audio>`/`<source>`, `<link>`, and `srcdoc=` intact), `MailStore` interpolated unsanitized attachment `contentType` into `data:` URLs (including `image/svg+xml`), `MailWebView` accepted `http://` navigations and lacked explicit `javaScriptCanOpenWindowsAutomatically = false`, and `temporaryFileURL()` did not strip `..` segments.

## Evidence

- `ios/LukaMail/Views/MailHTMLDocument.swift`: tag stripping covered only 4 tags and remote-image blocking left `http://`, `srcset`, `<link>`, `<video poster>`, and CSS `url(...)` paths open.
- `ios/LukaMail/Stores/MailStore.swift`: `inlineImages` interpolated raw `attachment.contentType` and `cid` without raster MIME allowlisting.
- `ios/LukaMail/Views/MailWebView.swift`, `Mac/Views/MailWebView.swift`, and `Shared/MailPresentation.swift`: navigation policy permitted `http://` links, `WKWebpagePreferences.allowsContentJavaScript` was only disabled for navigation actions rather than hardened with strict CSP + `mediaTypesRequiringUserActionForPlayback = .all`, and `temporaryFileURL()` left `..` in filenames.

## Risk

Tracking-pixel / remote-beacon leaks even with remote images blocked, base-URI hijacking of relative links, inline SVG markup injection in `data:` URLs, and path-component anomalies in Quick Look temporary files.

## Fix and current state

Fixed in `26f4d72` (`ios/LukaMail/Views/MailHTMLDocument.swift`, `Views/MailWebView.swift`, `Mac/Views/MailWebView.swift`, `Stores/MailStore.swift`, `Shared/MailPresentation.swift`, `Views/MessageDetailView.swift`, `Mac/Views/MacMessageDetailView.swift`): injected a defense-in-depth `<meta http-equiv="Content-Security-Policy">` inside `MailHTMLDocument`, stripped `<base>`, `<meta>`, `<svg>`, `<math>`, `<form>`, `<input>`, `<button>`, `<textarea>`, `<select>`, `<video>`, `<audio>`, `<source>`, `<track>`, `<portal>`, `<applet>`, `<frame>`, `<frameset>`, `<link>`, `on*=`, and `srcdoc=`, enforced HTTPS-only image loading (blocking `http://` even when remote images are enabled), restricted inline `cid:` `data:` URLs to raster `image/{png,jpeg,jpg,gif,webp,bmp,heic,heif,avif}`, restricted external link handoff to `https`/`mailto`, hardened `MailSafeFilename` + `temporaryFileURL()` (`0600`/`.completeFileProtection` writes), and filtered `spam == true` from inbox delta upserts.
