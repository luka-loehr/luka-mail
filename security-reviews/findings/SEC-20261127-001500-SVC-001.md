## Summary

Inbound sender trust (`isTrustedSender`), autopilot repeat collapsing (`collapseRepeats`/`tidyInbox`), and BIMI logo discovery (`senderPassedDmarc`) did not defeat forged `From:` headers or attacker-injected `Authentication-Results` comments/pre-existing headers, and `isTrustedSender` queried `contacts`/`messages` globally rather than per `identity_address`.

## Evidence

- `src/worker/bimi.ts`: `senderPassedDmarc` matched regexes across raw `Authentication-Results` headers without stripping RFC 5322 `(dmarc=pass)` comments or isolating the topmost MX header, and `parseBimiRecord` accepted cross-domain uncertified `l=` logo URLs.
- `src/worker/spam.ts` and `src/worker/inbound.ts`: `isTrustedSender` trusted any forged `From:` matching an outbound contact globally across identities without verifying SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
- `src/worker/autopilot.ts`: `collapseRepeats` archived older unread alerts from a sender address when an unauthenticated `Resolved:` spoof arrived.

## Risk

An unauthenticated sender spoofing a known correspondent or alert address could bypass Clef spam classification, display a third-party brand logo, or silently archive legitimate unread operational alerts in the inbox.

## Fix and current state

Fixed in `26f4d72` (`src/worker/bimi.ts`, `src/worker/inbound.ts`, `src/worker/external.ts`, `src/worker/spam.ts`, `src/worker/autopilot.ts`): added `stripHeaderComments` and topmost-header parsing for `senderPassedDmarc` and `senderPassedMailAuth` (requiring aligned `dmarc=pass` or aligned `dkim=pass`/`spf=pass` without DMARC failure), enforced domain alignment on uncertified BIMI `l=` URLs, scoped `isTrustedSender` per `identity_address`, and gated `trusted` and `collapseRepeats`/`tidyInbox` on `sender_authenticated = 1`.

## How to verify

- `npm test -- tests/security-hardening-v2.test.ts tests/bimi.test.ts tests/autopilot.test.ts`: verifies `(dmarc=pass)` comment injection fails closed, attacker-prepended `Authentication-Results` headers are ignored, cross-domain BIMI `l=` URLs are rejected unless VMC-certified, and unauthenticated spoofed `Resolved:` mails cannot archive authentic alerts.
