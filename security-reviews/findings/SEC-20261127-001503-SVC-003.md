## Summary

`fetchBytes` in `src/worker/logo-discovery.ts` used `redirect: "follow"`, allowing an initial HTTPS URL that passed `safeHttpsURL` to 302-redirect to `http://`, internal IP literals, or restricted hosts; `safeHttpsURL` also permitted arbitrary TCP ports and `.localhost`/`.local`/`.internal`/`.arpa` hostnames, and `safeLogoSVG` missed CSS backslash-escaped `u\72l(...)`, SMIL `<set>`/`<animate*>` elements, and `<use>` amplification.

## Evidence

- `src/worker/logo-discovery.ts`: `fetch(safe.toString(), { redirect: "follow" })` bypassed `safeHttpsURL` on redirect hops; `safeHttpsURL` did not restrict `parsed.port` or RFC 6761/8375 special-use suffixes; `safeLogoSVG` checked literal `url(` without rejecting `\` inside `<style>` or `style=` attributes.

## Risk

Blind SSRF / port probing on redirect hops during sender-logo discovery, unbounded parallel subrequests, and CSS/SVG evasion if a cached SVG were ever rendered outside a sandboxed `<img>` context.

## Fix and current state

Fixed in `26f4d72` (`src/worker/logo-discovery.ts`): replaced automatic redirect following with manual `safeFetch` (`redirect: "manual"`, max 3 hops, re-validating every `Location` target through `safeHttpsURL`), restricted `safeHttpsURL` to port 443/default and blocked IPv4/IPv6 literals plus `localhost`/`.localhost`/`.local`/`.internal`/`.invalid`/`.home.arpa`/`.arpa`, capped concurrent candidate probes at 4, and hardened `safeLogoSVG` against CSS `\` escapes, `<animate*>`/`<set>`, and `> 32` `<use>` elements while preserving `/sender-logo` `Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'none'; style-src 'unsafe-inline'; sandbox`.

## How to verify
