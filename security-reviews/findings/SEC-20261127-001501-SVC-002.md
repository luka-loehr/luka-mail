## Summary

Several `/v1/*` routes and background push/AI paths did not enforce the caller's `allowed_identities_json` or bind APNs device tokens to the issuing `native_sessions` row, allowing restricted per-mailbox keys or revoked native sessions to observe or trigger cross-identity operations.

## Evidence

- `src/worker/api.ts`: `POST /v1/external/sync`, `GET /v1/deliverability`, `GET /v1/autopilot`, `POST/PATCH/DELETE /v1/autopilot/rules*`, `GET /v1/keys`, and `GET /v1/audit` returned or mutated records outside a restricted key's `allowedIdentities`.
- `src/worker/push.ts` and `src/worker/api.ts`: `push_devices` was keyed only on `key_id` without `native_session_hash` or live `allowed_identities_json` re-evaluation during `sendNewMailPush`.
- `src/worker/reply-drafts.ts`: `earlierMails` fell back to `@domain` matches without filtering `identity_address`, leaking cross-identity subject/snippet context into AI draft prompts.

## Risk

Privilege escalation across delegated identities on multi-address deployments, residual APNs push metadata delivery after native device-session revocation, and cross-mailbox prompt context bleed.

## Fix and current state

Fixed in `26f4d72` (`migrations/0025_security_hardening_v2.sql`, `src/worker/api.ts`, `src/worker/external.ts`, `src/worker/push.ts`, `src/worker/reply-drafts.ts`): bound `push_devices.native_session_hash` with `ON DELETE CASCADE` to `native_sessions(token_hash)`, verified live `native_sessions` + `mail:read` + `allowed_identities_json` before APNs fan-out, isolated restricted keys across `/v1/external/sync`, `/v1/deliverability`, `/v1/autopilot*`, `/v1/keys`, and `/v1/audit`, and restricted `earlierMails` to exact `(identity_address, correspondent)` matches.

## How to verify
